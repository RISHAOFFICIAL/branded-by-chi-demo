What changes for Chi

01

New clients find her

Right now, the only way to find Chi is through Instagram. With a real website, Google searches for "Detroit braider," "knotless braids Detroit," and "stitch feedins near me" bring new clients straight to her booking page.

02

Updates take minutes

Adding a new hairstyle or changing a price shouldn't take an hour. The new site has a simple service menu that's easy to update. One place, one time, done.

03

Her work sells itself

A portfolio gallery with real photos of her braids, weaves, and feedins does more selling than any caption. Clients see the work, see the price, and book.

04

Trust before booking

Reviews from happy clients, a real About page, and professional branding tell new visitors: this is a serious business, not just someone braiding in their kitchen.

05

Booking stays easy

We keep Acuity for scheduling. Clients click "Book Now" and land right on her existing booking page. Nothing changes about how she manages appointments.

06

One link, everywhere

Instead of sending people to a plain booking page, Chi gets a website link she can put in her Instagram bio, share in DMs, and print on business cards. One link that does it all.
